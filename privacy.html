gws-cli privacy policy

gws-cli is a personal tool used only by its owner to access the owner's own Google account.

  • Data accessed through Google APIs is used only on the owner's own computer, to carry out commands the owner runs.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
